Chowly Mobile App Onboarding · iOS

Giving Chowly access to your App Store account

You already have an app on the App Store. Rather than publish a brand-new one, we update the app you have — so your guests keep the same icon on their home screen and simply get an update instead of being asked to download something new. To do that, we need to be added to your App Store Connect account. Here's exactly what to click.

About 15 minutes, one time Done by whoever owns your Apple Developer account 4 steps

Your guests keep your app

We reuse your existing app listing, bundle ID, and SKU. Everyone who already has your app gets it as an update — no re-download, no lost installs, no starting over at zero.

You stay the owner

The Apple Developer account remains yours, in your name, billed to you. We're added as a user on it, and you can remove our access at any time.

Your old provider comes off

If a previous app company is still listed on the account, this is the moment to remove them, so only you and Chowly can publish to your app.

Before you start

Four things need to be true. If any one of them isn't, tell your Chowly contact first — we'll sort it out with you.

  • An active Apple Developer Program membership. If it has lapsed, renew it before starting — an expired membership blocks new builds.
  • An existing iOS app that is live on the App Store now, or was live at some point.
  • Account Holder or Admin access to that App Store Connect account. If a previous agency set it up in their own name, we'll need to talk about transferring it to you.
  • Your Apple ID sign-in and two-factor device on hand — Apple will prompt for a verification code.

The steps

Four things to do in your Apple accounts

Steps 1 and 2 happen in App Store Connect. Steps 3 and 4 gather the four pieces of information we need to build and submit your app for you.

  1. 1
    App Store Connect · Users and Access

    Invite Chowly as an admin user

    This is what lets our team upload builds and manage your app listing on your behalf.

    1. Sign in at appstoreconnect.apple.com.
    2. Open Users and Access.
    3. Click Add User (the + button).
    4. Fill in the invitation:
      • First name: Platform
      • Last name: Chowly
      • Email: the Chowly address included in the email this guide came with
      • Role: Admin
      • Additional resources: check Access to Cloud Managed Developer ID Certificate
    5. Click Next, then Invite.

    Use the exact address we sent you. Apple ties the invitation to that address, so a typo or a different Chowly address means deleting the invite and starting over. If you can't find it, just reply and ask — we'll resend it.

  2. 2
    App Store Connect · Users and Access

    Remove your previous app developer

    If the company that built your old app still has a user on this account, take them off now. Skip this step if they were never on it.

    1. Still in Users and Access, find the previous developer's user.
    2. Check the box next to their name.
    3. Click Delete, then confirm.

    Any admin can remove any user except the Account Holder. If the old provider is listed as the Account Holder rather than an admin, stop here — that's an ownership transfer, not a user removal. Send us a screenshot of the Users and Access page and we'll walk you through it.

  3. 3
    App Store Connect · Integrations

    Create an API key for Chowly

    The key is what allows our build system to submit new versions of your app automatically. You can create it yourself, or the Chowly admin you invited in step 1 can do it once their invitation is accepted.

    1. Go to Users and Access › Integrations.
    2. Click the + to generate a new key.
    3. Enter the details and generate it:
      • Name: ChowlyAPIkey
      • Access: Admin
    4. On the confirmation screen, capture all three of these before leaving the page:
      What to copyLooks like
      Key ID ABC123DEF4 — a short 10-character code
      Issuer ID 12345678-1234-1234-1234-123456789012 — one per account
      Private key file A download named AuthKey_ABC123DEF4.p8

    The .p8 file can only be downloaded once

    Apple will not let you download it a second time. Download it, save it somewhere you won't lose it, and don't close the page until you've confirmed the file is on your computer. If it does get lost, it isn't the end of the world — you'd revoke that key and generate a new one, then repeat this step.

  4. 4
    developer.apple.com · Membership Details

    Copy your Team ID

    This is the last piece. It lives in the Apple Developer portal, not App Store Connect.

    1. Sign in at developer.apple.com/account.
    2. Open Membership Details.
    3. Copy the Team ID — a 10-character code such as A1B2C3D4E5.

Step 5, sort of

Send us these four items

Once we have all four, nothing else is needed from you until it's time to review the app before submission.

KEY_ID

The 10-character key identifier from step 3.

ISSUER_ID

The long dashed ID from the same screen.

TEAM_ID

From Membership Details in step 4.

AuthKey_….p8

The downloaded private key file itself.

How to send them

Reply to the email this guide came with. Paste the three IDs into the message and attach the .p8 file. Keep it to that thread rather than forwarding it on elsewhere — the .p8 is the credential that lets software publish to your app, so the fewer inboxes it lands in, the better.

What happens next

Your part is done. Here's ours.

We build your app

Using your existing bundle ID, we build your Chowly-powered app — your menu, your branding, your ordering — and connect it to the online ordering you already run with us.

We prepare the new version

We create the new version in your App Store listing, replace the old provider's screenshots with new ones, and write the "What's New in This Version" note your guests will see.

You review before it ships

We'll walk you through the build before anything is submitted to Apple, so you see exactly what your guests will get.

Apple reviews, then it goes live

We submit it and monitor the review. Apple review timelines vary; if they come back with questions, we handle the response and keep you posted.
