Why it works

Four things your ads do for you

Search Ads aren't awareness advertising. They reach people who are already looking for food like yours, in your neighborhood, right now.

Intent, not impressions

You reach hungry people, not everyone

Your ad shows when someone searches a term that fits your food — “pizza near me,” “best ramen downtown” — inside the area around your store. You're not paying to interrupt anyone. You're paying to be the answer to a question they already asked.

The map pack

You win the top spot on Google Maps

When your Google Business Profile is linked to your ad account, your ad can appear as a sponsored result inside Maps — with Order Online, Directions, and Call buttons attached. That placement sits above every other nearby restaurant competing for the same search.

Direct orders

Traffic goes to your site, not a marketplace

Every ad points straight at your own online ordering page. The goal isn't to move someone off DoorDash after they've opened it — it's to reach them first, so the order never carries a 15–30% commission in the first place.

Compounding value

Your ad data makes your whole site rank better

The search terms your ads win tell us exactly how people in your neighborhood look for food like yours. We feed that language back into your website copy, image tags, and local SEO — so your ads improve what you get from Google for free, too.

How your ads are built

Every location gets its own campaign — never one generic ad spread across the brand.

One campaign per location

Its own ad, its own ordering link, its own performance you can read on its own.

Targeted to your trade area

A radius around the store, specific ZIP codes, or your metro area — whichever fits how people actually travel to you.

Linked to your Business Profile

This is what puts your ad on Maps and adds the Directions and Call buttons.

Always on, always learning

Running continuously lets Google learn who's most likely to click. Headlines and descriptions are tested against each other automatically.

What that buys, and how we'll measure it

  • You only pay for clicks. Showing up in a search costs nothing. You're charged when someone actually taps your ad.
  • Google won't overspend. Your daily budget is the ceiling, and you can change it any day.
  • First read at 2–4 weeks. That's enough runtime to report on clicks, cost per click, and total spend, and to make the first real budget call.
  • Steady beats bursts. Spreading budget over time gets you seen more than once, which is what moves a diner from noticing you to choosing you.
  • Order-level revenue tracking is a separate step. The ads report clicks and cost by default. To tie specific online orders and dollar amounts back to an ad, we install Google conversion tracking on your site — ask us to set that up if you want revenue attribution.
